Patient help. Clear answers.
No tech jargon.

Technology should make life easier—not more stressful. Good Neighbor Tech is a not-for-profit community service built to be helpful, much like volunteering: helping local families and neighbors solve technology problems without treating them like customers of a traditional tech business.

Not-for-profit community service

Tech help by donation

Good Neighbor Tech is not-for-profit and donation-supported. This is something I do to be helpful—more like volunteering than running a traditional tech business. There are no fixed service prices. If you are able to donate, your contribution helps cover supplies, parts, tools, transportation, and the time it takes to help another neighbor.

💚

Give What You Can

There is no fixed price for help. If you can contribute, great; if not, please still reach out. The goal is to be useful to people who need a hand.

👵

Special Care for Seniors

I especially enjoy working with seniors and taking the time to explain technology clearly, patiently, and without jargon.

🤝

Local Families First

The focus is on individuals, seniors, and local families—not business accounts. Donations help keep this community service going and help put useful technology back into people’s hands.

The experience behind the help

Technology is more than a job to me.

I have hands-on experience supporting computers, software, networks, accounts, email, and everyday technology. My professional background includes working as a Computer Support Specialist, Technical Care Support Specialist, and in technology-focused roles.

I earned my Bachelor of Science in Business Administration from St. Petersburg College, and I hold CompTIA certification. I bring that professional experience to Good Neighbor Tech because I enjoy using technology skills to help real people in my community.

🎓

From professional tech support to community service

My goal here is simple: use the skills I have built over the years to make technology less frustrating and more accessible for neighbors who need a little help.

♻️
Keep useful technology out of the waste stream

Repair it. Reuse it. Recycle it responsibly.

I believe old technology can still have value. When a device can be repaired or reused, I try to keep it in service. When it cannot, I look for responsible electronics recycling options. St. Petersburg College’s Gadgets for Good initiative has a special connection to this mission: SPC has partnered with eSmart Recycling on efforts to collect used electronics, destroy data, recycle devices, and expand access to technology in the community.
